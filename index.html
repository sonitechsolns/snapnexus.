Features

Everything the night needs. Nothing it doesn't.

No app, no sign-up

Guests scan with their normal camera and upload from the browser. Nothing to install and no password to forget, so more people actually share.

Live on any screen

Smart TV, laptop on a projector, LED wall. If it has a browser, it can run your wall. New photos appear on their own.

You stay in control

Every upload lands in your dashboard in real time. Hide anything from the wall or delete it in one tap.

Private groups

Bridesmaids, the college gang, the core team: guests can start a passcode-locked group. Those photos stay off the big screen and in your download.

Photos and short videos

Guests can send up to 5 at a time, including clips up to 15 seconds. Every photo is stamped with your event name, so shared pictures carry your moment with it.

One-click ZIP

When the party's over, download every photo from every guest in a single ZIP. No chasing people on group chats.

FAQ

Good questions.

Do my guests need to download an app?

No. Guests scan the QR code with their phone's camera and SnapNexus opens in the browser. No app, no account, no OTP.

What do I need for the live wall?

Any screen that can open a web page: a smart TV, a laptop connected to a projector or LED wall, even a tablet. Open your event's wall link and leave it running.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Every new host gets 1 hour of live wall time for free. Nothing to pay and no card needed. Trial walls show ads. Upgrade to a paid plan to remove them.

Can I see it working before I sign up?

Yes. Open the live demo on your laptop, then scan the QR code on it with your phone. Your photos appear on the demo wall.

How do I pay?

By UPI. When you upgrade, you'll see a UPI QR code you can pay from GPay, PhonePe, Paytm or any UPI app. Then enter the UPI reference number, and we switch your plan on as soon as the payment is confirmed. That's usually within a few hours.

What if someone uploads something inappropriate?

You'll see every upload in your dashboard as it arrives. Hide it from the wall or delete it with one tap.

How do I get the photos after the event?

From your dashboard, download the whole gallery as a single ZIP file with one click. Then share it with the family, the client, or your photographer.
